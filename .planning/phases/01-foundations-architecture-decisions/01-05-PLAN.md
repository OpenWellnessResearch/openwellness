Purpose: Postgres (Phase 4) can implement `get_by_email` without a query dialect, and registration keeps its anti-enumeration guarantees on any backend.
Output: The `UserRepository.get_by_email` port and Mongo implementation, `DuplicateEntityException` with a 409 mapping, and updated auth service and tests.
</objective>
